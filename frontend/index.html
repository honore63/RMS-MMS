RMS-MIS

Rukara Model School Marks Information System

Secure Academic Records. Accurate Results. Better Management.

A secure digital platform designed to support the management of assessments, student marks, results, approvals, and academic reports.

Key Functions

Assessment Management — Create and manage academic assessments.
Marks Management — Record, import, review, and process student marks.
Results Management — Review, approve, lock, and publish official results.
Academic Reports — Generate professional and printable school reports.
Secure Access — Role-based access for administrators, DOS, and teachers.
Academic Analytics — Monitor performance and access meaningful academic information.
